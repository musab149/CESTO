## 2. What we collect, and why

We only collect what the service needs. Each item below says what it is for and how
long we keep it.

### 2.1 Account details

**What:** your name, email address, phone number, and a password (stored only as a
salted hash — we never hold the password itself and cannot recover it for you).

**Why:** to create and secure your account, to identify you when you sign in, and to
contact you about an order.

**Legal basis:** performance of a contract.

### 2.2 Delivery addresses

**What:** street address, postcode, city, and any delivery note you add.

**Why:** to deliver your order and to work out whether we deliver to your area.

**Legal basis:** performance of a contract.

### 2.3 Orders

**What:** the products you bought, quantities, prices, order number, date, status, the
chosen payment method, and delivery history.

**Why:** to fulfil the order, to show you your order history, to handle returns and
refunds, and to meet our accounting and tax obligations.

**Legal basis:** performance of a contract, and legal obligation for the accounting
records.

### 2.4 Reviews you write

**What:** your star rating, review title and text, and the first name shown alongside
it. A review can only be written for a product that has actually been delivered to
you.

**Why:** to show other customers what people thought of a product.

**Legal basis:** performance of a contract for the link to your order; legitimate
interest in operating a review system.

**Note:** reviews are **public**. Your review appears with your first name to anyone
using Cesto. Please do not put personal information in a review. You can delete your own
review at any time from the product page.

### 2.5 Photos you upload

**What:** a profile picture, if you choose to add one. If you hold a partner (shop)
account, also the product and shop images you upload.

**Why:** to display your profile, and to display your shop and products.

**Legal basis:** consent, which you give by choosing to upload. Remove the image at
any time to withdraw it.

### 2.6 Device location

**What:** your device's approximate position, only when you tap the location control
and only while the app is open.

**Why:** solely to work out which town and postcode you are in, so the app can show
whether we deliver there.

**Important:** we convert the position into a town and postcode **on your device**.
The coordinates are **not sent to our servers and are not stored by us**. To perform
that conversion, the coordinates are sent to Google's geocoding service, which acts
under its own privacy policy.

We never collect location in the background. The app has no background-location
permission.

**Legal basis:** consent, given through the operating-system permission prompt. You
can withdraw it in your device settings at any time; only the location shortcut stops
working.

### 2.7 Push notifications

**What:** a notification token issued to your device by Google (Firebase Cloud
Messaging), and your notification preferences.

**Why:** to tell you that an order was confirmed, is on its way, or has arrived, and
to send service messages.

**Legal basis:** consent, given through the notification permission prompt. Turn it
off in your device settings at any time.

### 2.8 Support messages

**What:** whatever you send us — your message, your contact details, and any order it
refers to.

**Why:** to answer you and to keep a record of the issue.

**Legal basis:** performance of a contract, and legitimate interest in keeping support
records.

### 2.9 Technical and security logs

**What:** IP address, request timestamps, and error information recorded by our
servers.

**Why:** to keep the service running, to investigate faults, and to detect abuse.

**Legal basis:** legitimate interest in the security and reliability of the service.

## 3. Payment information — what we do *not* collect

**Cesto does not take payment in the app or on the website.** Every order is paid on
delivery, by one of:

- **cash** to the delivery rider; or
- **card**, using a card reader the rider brings.

We store only **which method you chose** and whether the order was paid. We never
receive, process or store card numbers, card security codes or bank account
details. There is no payment gateway integrated into the app, and no
cardholder data ever reaches our systems.

---

## 4. What we do *not* do

We say this plainly because it is unusual, and because it should be checkable:

- **No advertising.** The app contains no advertising and no ad network.
- **No third-party analytics or tracking.** We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase
  Analytics, Meta SDK, or any comparable product. We do not build advertising profiles
  and we do not use advertising identifiers.
- **No third-party crash or session-recording tools.**
- **We do not sell personal data**, and we do not share it for advertising.

If this ever changes, we will update this policy and, where the law requires it, ask
for your consent first.

## 7. How long we keep it

| Data | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Account details | while your account is open |
| Addresses | while your account is open, or until you delete them |
| Orders and invoices | **the period required by Spanish tax and commercial law**, currently at least 4 years, even after you close your account |
| Reviews | until you delete the review or your account |
| Uploaded photos | until you remove them or delete your account |
| Support messages | up to 3 years after the issue is closed |
| Technical and security logs | up to 12 months |

When we no longer need data, we delete it or anonymise it so it can no longer be
linked to you.

## 8. Deleting your account

You can delete your Cesto account yourself, at any time:

**In the app:** Profile → Edit profile → **Delete account**
**On the web:** www.cestoshop.com → Profile → Edit profile → **Delete account**

Or email **support.cesto@gmail.com** and we will do it for you.

When you delete your account we remove your name, contact details, addresses, profile
photo, notification token and reviews.

**What survives, and why:** we are legally required to keep invoices and order records
for tax purposes. Those records are **anonymised** — detached from your name and
contact details — and kept only for as long as the law requires.

## 9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you, and receive a copy;
- **correct** anything inaccurate or incomplete;
- **delete** your data, in the circumstances the law provides;
- **restrict** how we process it, in certain circumstances;
- **port** your data to another provider in a machine-readable format;
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interests, and at any time to direct
  marketing;
- **withdraw consent** where processing relies on it, without affecting what was done
  before.

To exercise any of these, email **support.cesto@gmail.com**. We may ask you to confirm
your identity, and we will reply **within one month**, as the GDPR requires.

We do not make decisions about you by solely automated means that produce legal or
similarly significant effects.

## 11. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit (HTTPS), password hashing, access
controls restricting staff access to what their role requires, server and application
hardening, logging and monitoring, and regular backups.

No online service can promise absolute security, but if a breach occurs that is likely
to put your rights at risk, we will notify the AEPD within 72 hours and tell you
directly where the law requires it.

## 12. Children

Cesto is for adults who can enter into a contract. It is not directed at children, and
we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us
personal data, write to **support.cesto@gmail.com** and we will remove it.

unsubscribe. Withdrawing consent stops them without affecting your account.
